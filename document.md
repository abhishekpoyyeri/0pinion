# 0pinion

## Product Vision

0pinion is a monochrome, text-first platform built around opinions, debates, and knowledge exchange.

Unlike traditional social media platforms that focus on photos, videos, creators, and follower counts, 0pinion focuses exclusively on ideas.

The platform aims to replace passive doomscrolling with active participation by creating a space where opinions are challenged, refined, and debated.

---

# Tagline

Debate, Not Doomscroll.

---

# Mission

Create the internet's best place for structured opinions and meaningful debate.

---

# Problem

Modern social platforms are optimized for:

* Short-form content
* Endless scrolling
* Creator worship
* Engagement farming
* Visual stimulation

As a result:

* Users consume more than they contribute.
* Discussions are shallow.
* Valuable opinions are buried.
* Popularity often matters more than ideas.

---

# Solution

0pinion removes distractions and focuses entirely on discussion.

The platform includes:

* Opinion posts
* Structured debates
* Anonymous participation
* Live text discussions
* Topic communities called Zeroes

No photos.

No videos.

No stories.

No reels.

No follower system.

No influencer culture.

---

# Core Principles

## Ideas Over People

Opinions should be judged on quality, not popularity.

Users cannot follow each other.

---

## Debate Over Engagement

The goal is meaningful discussion, not maximizing screen time.

---

## Monochrome Experience

The entire platform uses a black-and-white design system.

No attention-grabbing colors.

No visual distractions.

---

## Privacy First

Users may participate anonymously.

---

## Community Driven

Content is organized around interests rather than personalities.

---

# Core Concepts

## Opinions

The primary content type.

Example:

"AI will replace most junior developers within ten years."

Every opinion becomes a debate.

---

## Debate Zones

Every opinion contains three sections.

### Support

Arguments supporting the opinion.

### Oppose

Arguments challenging the opinion.

### Question

Clarifications and inquiries.

---

## Zeroes

Zeroes are topic communities.

Examples:

* 0technology
* 0startup
* 0science
* 0gaming
* 0sports
* 0politics

Users join Zeroes to discover relevant discussions.

---

## Cooking

Cooking is the platform's version of trending.

Opinions enter Cooking when debate activity increases rapidly.

Signals include:

* High participation
* Fast reply growth
* Increasing engagement

Cooking represents discussions that are currently heating up.

---

## Live Rooms

Real-time text-only discussion rooms.

Users can:

* Join rooms
* Participate in discussions
* Debate with others

No voice.

No video.

No screen sharing.

Profiles do not display:

* Following
* Popularity metrics

Reputation reflects contribution quality.
