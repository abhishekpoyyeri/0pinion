# 0pinion App Flow

Version: 2.0

---

# Core Philosophy

Users do not follow people.

Users follow ideas.

Discovery is driven by:

* Zeroes
* Debates
* Cooking
* Search
* Live Rooms

The platform is designed to move users from consuming opinions to participating in discussions.

# Opinion Detail Flow

Opinion contains:

* Title
* Content
* Author
* Zeroes
* Timestamp

Below opinion:

### Support

Arguments supporting the opinion.

### Oppose

Arguments challenging the opinion.

### Question

Clarifications and inquiries.
