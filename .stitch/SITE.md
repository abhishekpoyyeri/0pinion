## 1. Vision
0pinion is a monochrome, text-first mobile debate platform. Ideas over people, debates over engagement, thinking over scrolling.

## 3. Tech Stack
- Flutter + Dart (Mobile App)
- Riverpod (State Management)
- Supabase (Backend, Auth, Realtime)
- PostgreSQL (Database)
- Firebase FCM (Push Notifications)
