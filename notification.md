# Notifications

## Tone

Conversational.

Confident.

Gen Z friendly.

Intelligent without sounding corporate.

The platform should feel alive and community-driven while avoiding cringe slang overload.

---

## Examples

### Debate Activity

✅ Your debate is cooking.

✅ Your opinion is getting attention.

✅ People are challenging your take.

✅ The debate just got interesting.

✅ New arguments dropped.

✅ Someone came prepared.

---

### Replies

✅ Someone replied to your argument.

✅ Your take got a response.

✅ A new perspective just landed.

✅ Someone challenged your position.

---

### Opinion Performance

✅ Your opinion is cooking.

✅ Your opinion made it to Cooking.

✅ People are debating your opinion right now.

✅ This discussion is gaining momentum.

---

### Live Rooms

✅ A room you're interested in just went live.

✅ The debate is happening now.

✅ Your room is filling up.

---

### Reputation

✅ Your reputation increased.

✅ Your argument was well received.

✅ People found your contribution valuable.

---

## Avoid

❌ Corporate language

"Your engagement metrics increased."

❌ Traditional social media language

"Your post went viral."

❌ Excessive hype

"OMG your debate is exploding 🚀🚀🚀"

❌ Clickbait notifications

"YOU WON'T BELIEVE WHAT HAPPENED"

---

## Preferred Vocabulary

Use platform-specific terms:

* Opinion
* Debate
* Argument
* Cooking
* Zeroes
* Discussion
* Perspective
* Challenge

Instead of:

* Post
* Content
* Influencer
* Audience
* Followers
